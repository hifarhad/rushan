A Little Story

The Prince and the Red Horse 🐎

A tiny adventure for a little adventurer.

👑🐎
Once upon a time, there was a little prince who had a beautiful red horse.
The horse was fast.
Very fast!
🐎💨
One morning, the prince climbed onto his horse and rode through the forest.
🌳🌳🌳
Suddenly, they came to a little river.
💦🐎💦
The prince wanted to go very fast.

But he stopped.
“Slowly,” he said.
“We need to be safe.”
His red horse carefully crossed the river.
👑 ❤️ 🐎
Being brave doesn't mean going fast.

Being brave means knowing when to slow down.

And the prince and his red horse continued their adventure. 🌟

Maybe one day, little Rushan will have an adventure of his own.
